# Official university logo sources

Downloaded 15 October 2026 for the hero carousel. Original files are preserved alongside display derivatives.

- Sapienza: https://www.uniroma1.it/ — inline header SVG, original burgundy seal and white cherub details.
- University of Bologna: https://www.unibo.it/it/++theme++unibotheme.portale/img/header/sigillo1x_xl.png?v=2
- Politecnico di Milano: https://www.polimi.it/_assets/4b51f00386267395f41e0940abbcd656/Images/logo.svg
- University of Padua: https://wwwassets.unipd.it/sites/default/files/media/2026-04/UNIPD_Marchio_STD_RED.png
- Politecnico di Torino: https://www.polito.it/themes/custom/polito_customizations/polito_logo_mobile.svg?v=1764328516

Display derivatives show the emblem portion of the official artwork. SVG derivatives adjust the viewport; raster derivatives crop the emblem, preserve source alpha and colours, and resize to a maximum 160 pixels as lossless WebP. No colour replacement, background-removal filter, or redrawing is applied.
