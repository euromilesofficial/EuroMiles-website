START WITH THE ESSENTIALS

Two paths. One exciting opportunity.

Imagine studying in Italy at a university that fits your ambitions and your family’s budget. Whether you choose a public or private university, your journey starts with finding the right course—and discovering the financial support available to you.

Public universities bring respected names like Bologna, Sapienza, Padua and Politecnico di Milano within reach. Explore accessible tuition and scholarships that can support eligible students with fees, meals and living costs. With a full tuition waiver, your tuition can be €0.

Private universities offer another exciting route, with institutions such as Bocconi, Cattolica, LUISS and Humanitas. Fees are often higher, but eligible students can explore scholarships and financial support here too. Legally recognised private university qualifications have the same legal value in Italy as those from state universities. Learn more about Italian higher education.

UNDERSTANDING FUNDING

Three scholarship layers

Scholarships can bring your Italian education within reach. Explore three routes to funding:

Regional right to study

Explore regional funding for eligible students, with benefits that can include tuition support, a stipend, meals or accommodation. Benefits vary by region and award.

University awards

Discover university scholarships, tuition waivers and fee reductions based on the institution’s academic and financial criteria.

Government and other grants

Government and independent organisations offer additional scholarships for eligible programmes and applicants. Check each call for its benefits and deadlines.

Explore scholarships at both public and private universities. Each award has its own eligibility rules and benefits. For example, Bocconi’s 2026–27 ISU scholarship is a regional right-to-study opportunity for eligible students; its conditions and coverage should be checked carefully.

For applicants with family income or assets abroad, the required financial documents and any translation or legalisation depend on the relevant institution or regional call. Confirm the exact requirements before submitting. Universitaly explains the different types of support.

YOUR DECISION

Which should you choose?

Your dream university is the one that fits your goals, budget and academic background. Start with the right course—then explore the funding that can make it possible.

  • Compare the programme: course content, teaching language, entry requirements, accreditation and career opportunities.
  • Calculate the full cost: tuition, compulsory charges and living expenses, less only confirmed funding.
  • Check scholarship rules: deadlines, income and asset documents, merit requirements and when benefits are paid.
  • Look at the experience: city, accommodation, class size, student support and opportunities relevant to your goals.

NEXT STEPS

A checklist for Indian applicants

  1. Shortlist recognised institutions and coursesCheck the qualification, language of instruction, subject prerequisites and the university’s published entry requirements.
  2. Check the application and funding calendarsUniversity and scholarship applications often follow separate processes and deadlines. Check the current call for your intake.
  3. Prepare the documentsFollow the university’s instructions for academic records, language proof and any required evaluation or financial documents.
  4. Plan pre-enrolment and your visaWhere applicable, complete the Universitaly pre-enrolment process and follow the competent Italian mission’s visa instructions. University validation does not guarantee a visa. Read the official steps.

THE DETAILS, MADE CLEAR

Questions Indian families ask us

Straightforward answers to help you take the next step. Still unsure? Get a free profile assessment.

Can I study in Italy with zero tuition?

Yes. Eligible students who receive a full tuition waiver can study with zero tuition fees. Each award has its own requirements and availability. Check what is included: taxes, other fees and living costs can still apply.

Are Italian degrees recognised in India?

Recognition or equivalence is not automatic. Check the requirements of the Indian university, employer or relevant professional regulator for your specific qualification and purpose. The Association of Indian Universities publishes guidance on eligibility for foreign-degree equivalence.

Can I get a scholarship at a private university?

Yes. Private universities offer their own awards, and eligible students can also explore right-to-study benefits at participating institutions. Check the institution’s and region’s current calls, including eligibility, coverage and deadlines.

Can I study in Italy without speaking Italian?

Some programmes are taught in English. Check the language of your chosen course and its accepted language qualifications. Learning Italian can also be useful for everyday life.

Is a public university always the better choice?

Not necessarily. Compare the particular course, total cost after confirmed aid, location, teaching and career fit. Both public and legally recognised private universities can offer valuable options.

Does university admission guarantee a student visa?

University admission is an important step. The visa decision is made separately by the competent Italian diplomatic or consular mission.
